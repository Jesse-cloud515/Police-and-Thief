    HOW TO PLAY

    Police 🚓 Get close to a thief and you grab automatically; they get a split second to dodge. Caught thieves go to the Police Post. Catch everyone to win. Whistle 📯 slows every thief near you and pulls them out of hiding. Don't hang around inside the orange ring: Oga reassigns campers.

    Thieves 🏃 Hide under trees and canopies (sprinting gives you away). Hold FREE at a gate of the Post to release everyone. Dash 💨 is a quick burst that can beat a grab. When the four corner exits open at 2:00, hold ESCAPE inside one. Two escapes and the thieves win.

    Street money and shops ₦ Pick up the green ₦ notes, then stand at a shop counter and tap BUY: jollof at Mama Put, pure water at Iya Bisi, suya, glucose, kunu, small chops. Each one helps your running. Free Agege bread and zobo also turn up on the street.

    Locations 📍 Pick where to play on the home screen. Naija Street has the football field and cattle crossing the road. Market Day is a maze of stalls with cheaper food and more ₦. In the School Yard, ring the bell to see the other team on your map. Behind the Church has tall grass to hide in and a hole in the fence that takes you across the compound.

    Football ⚽ There's a ball on the field. Run into it to kick it, even mid-chase. Put it between the posts for ₦30 and bragging rights.

    Night games 🌙 "NEPA took light": the police see less and carry torches.

    Controls Use the visible joystick on the lower-left to move. RUN toggles sprint. Keyboard: WASD, Shift, Space, Q.

    Win games, finish daily missions and keep your streak to earn ₦ and XP for new outfits and ranks.
