# Catch Me If You Fit

Theme song for Police & Thief: Naija Streets.

- Instrumental: `audio/theme.mp3` (1:32, loops cleanly). It plays in the menu and, quieter, during matches.
- Key A minor, 104 BPM. Chords all the way through, one bar each: **Am | F | C | G**
- The whistle in the choruses carries the tune a singer would take. A singer can go straight over this track, or a producer can rebuild it from `tools/make_theme.py`, where every note is written out.
- Layout of the track: intro (4 bars), chorus (8), verse (8), chorus (8), chant break (4), last chorus (8).

Chorus tune, first two lines, in solfa (doh = C): `m m r d l, – d r m | d d l, s, l, – d r`

## Lyrics

**Chorus**

Catch me if you fit, you no fit
I don pass the gutter, you still dey count
Free my guys for gate, make we waka
Before your mama call you go house

**Verse 1 (thief)**

Four o'clock, sun never go down
Slippers for hand, na so we dey run am
Tunde say na him be police today
Last week na me. I never forgive am

Mango tree, na there I dey hide
Chicken dey shout, e wan cast me
Mama Put see me, she just dey laugh
"Small thief, your jollof go cold o"

**Chorus**

**Verse 2 (police)**

Whistle for mouth, I count reach five
Eye half open, abeg no tell dem

The one wey dey kick ball, na him I go carry first

Oga say make I no stand for gate

**Last chorus**, then:

NEPA take light, game no stop
